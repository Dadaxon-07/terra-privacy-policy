TERRA Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

TERRA is a real-world exploration game that allows users to explore locations, track their movement, and claim virtual territories on a map.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: Account identifier, name, and email address provided through sign-in.
  • Location information: Device location when location features are enabled.
  • Usage and game data: Territory ownership, distance walked, gameplay activity, and progress.
  • Device and technical information: Technical logs and diagnostic information.

How We Use Information

We use information to operate the game, authenticate users, display territories, synchronize progress, improve performance, prevent abuse, and respond to support requests.

Sharing of Information

TERRA may use Firebase and related service providers for authentication, database storage, synchronization, and app reliability. We do not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect the service.

Location Permissions

Location access supports location-based gameplay. You can manage permissions through your device settings. Some features may not work without location access.

Account and Data Deletion

Users can request deletion of their TERRA account and associated personal data.

  1. Email pragramist07@gmail.com with the subject TERRA Account Deletion Request.
  2. Include the email address associated with your TERRA account.
  3. We may contact you to verify the request.

After verification, we will delete the account and associated personal data we are not required or permitted to retain. Some information may be retained for legal obligations, dispute resolution, or security purposes. We will provide further instructions by email.

Data Retention

We retain information only as long as necessary to provide the game and meet applicable legal obligations. Eligible data will be deleted when the request is processed, subject to applicable retention requirements.

Children's Privacy

TERRA is not specifically designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

Security

We use appropriate measures to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.

Contact
