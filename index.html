# TERRA Privacy Policy **Last updated: October 10, 2026** TERRA is a real-world exploration game that allows users to explore locations, track their movement, and claim virtual territories on a map. ## Information We Collect Depending on how you use TERRA, we may collect: - **Account information:** Information provided through sign-in, such as your account identifier, name, and email address. - **Location information:** Device location when location features are enabled, to support map exploration, movement tracking, and virtual territory features. - **Usage and game data:** Territory ownership, distance walked, gameplay activity, and related progress. - **Device and technical information:** Technical logs and diagnostic information needed to maintain app functionality and security. ## How We Use Information We use information to provide and maintain the game, authenticate users, display territories on the map, synchronize game progress, improve performance, prevent abuse, and respond to support requests. ## Sharing of Information TERRA may use service providers, including Firebase and its related services, for authentication, database storage, synchronization, and app reliability. Information may be processed by these providers as necessary to deliver the service and in accordance with their applicable terms and privacy practices. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information when required by law or when necessary to protect the security of users and the service. ## Location Permissions Location access is used to support location-based gameplay. You can manage location permissions through your device settings. Some game features may not work without location access. ## Account and Data Deletion Users can request deletion of their TERRA account and associated personal data. To request account deletion: 1. Data eligible for deletion will be deleted when the account deletion request is processed, subject to applicable retention requirements. ## Children's Privacy TERRA is not specifically designed for children under 13.
