TERRA Account and Data Deletion

This page explains how TERRA users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to pragramist07@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject: TERRA Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address associated with your TERRA account.
  4. We may contact you to verify your identity before processing the request.

What Data Is Deleted?

After verifying your request, we will delete your account and associated personal data, including account information and game progress, where applicable and where we are not required to retain it.

Data That May Be Retained

Some information may be retained when required by law or necessary for security, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution. Any retained information will be limited to what is necessary for that purpose.

Deletion Time

We will review your request and communicate with you by email about the verification and deletion process.

Contact

For account deletion questions, contact pragramist07@gmail.com.
