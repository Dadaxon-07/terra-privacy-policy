TERRA Account and Data Deletion

TERRA users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Email pragramist07@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject: TERRA Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address associated with your TERRA account.
  4. We may contact you to verify your identity.

What Data Is Deleted?

After verification, we will delete your account and associated personal data, including account information and game progress, where applicable.

Data That May Be Retained

Some information may be retained when required by law or necessary for security or dispute resolution.

Deletion Time

We will communicate with you by email about the verification and deletion process.

Contact

For deletion requests, email pragramist07@gmail.com.
